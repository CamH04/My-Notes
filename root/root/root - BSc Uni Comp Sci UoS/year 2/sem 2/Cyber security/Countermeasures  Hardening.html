Countermeasures / Hardening

Both: actions that you take to off-set a problem of an attack that could take place 

 

Countermeasures:how you protect the services that you need to run to provide the infrastructure requirements

 

Hardening: Is about removing the potential attack vectors that do not need to exist, such as removing services and facilities from a machine that are not being used.

 

TLDR:

Hardening normally involves removing facilities.
Countermeasures normally involve configuring services etc.
