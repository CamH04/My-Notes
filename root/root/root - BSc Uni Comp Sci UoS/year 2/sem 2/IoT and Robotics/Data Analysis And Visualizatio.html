Data Analysis And Visualization

DESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS
- Descriptive Analytics answer the generic question "what happened?" by    leveraging summary statistics (like average, median, and variance) and simple transformations and aggregations

 

PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

answering the natural follow-up questions that you have after learning what happened in the past, such as: "why did it happen?" and "what will happen now?".

eg: Statistical Prediction and forcasting

 

PRESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS

transforms data into a recommended course of action, by answering the ultimate : "what should be done?".
